# Sources and further study

Reference material for the project as a whole: primary texts, the systematic
works that make the vocabulary legible, lectures, and what to buy. Sources tied
to one parasha's passage sit at the end, under that parasha's heading.

Collected 2026-11-22, after teaching the Vayishlach מנחה material at Heimish.
Prices and availability were read on that date — confirm before ordering.

## Primary text

- **ליקוטי תורה להאריז"ל** — our passage in its own volume, without the Chumash
  edition's page breaks. Free scan on HebrewBooks (ID **30734** per Hamichlol —
  unverified; printings Zolkiew 1775, Amsterdam 1802, Vilna 1870).
- There is **no מתוק מדבש-style commentary on ליקוטי תורה**, which is why this
  passage has no crutch and the editor's footnotes keep landing on צ"ע. Frish
  does cover some Arizal writings — e.g. שער הגלגולים עם פירוש מתוק מדבש.

## The system — so the vocabulary stops being opaque

- **פתחי שערים**, R' Yitzchak Isaac Chaver — the systematic map of partzufim
  and mochin. The standard answer to "what does ק"ג on the left actually mean".
  Terse; there is a modern running beiur, **פירוש בלבבי משכן אבנה**.
- **קל"ח פתחי חכמה** (רמח"ל) — first principles, clearest starting point.
- **לשם שבו ואחלמה** (ר' שלמה אלישיב) — the deep end, later.
- In English, for students: *Mystical Concepts in Chassidism* (Schochet),
  *Innerspace* (Kaplan).

## English on the parasha

**Apples from the Orchard**, R' Moshe Wisnefsky (Thirty Seven Books, ~1,100
pp., $105–125) — translation **with commentary**, culled chiefly from
שער הפסוקים, ספר הליקוטים and ליקוטי תורה, i.e. our edition's own sources. It
has a VaYishlach section; whether that section includes this מנחה passage is
**unverified**.

## Vayishlach — `ויקח מן הבא בידו מנחה` (Gen 32:14)

Depth on the two things that passage assumes rather than explains:
**שי עולמות** and the right/left (ר"ז / ק"ג) split.

### The Zohar behind the opening — read this first

The Arizal cites **זוהר תרומה קס"ו** with `כבר ידעת` and builds everything on
it. In the standard pagination that is Zohar vol. 2, פרשת תרומה, דף קס"ו
(online chapter divisions: Trumah ch. 75). Mark has **מתוק מדבש**, which
carries the Zohar pagination in the margins, and **שערי זוהר** (R' Daniel
Frish's introduction). What that page adds beyond our passage:

- the ש"י עולמות are concealed beneath the World to Come, i.e. **beneath
  Binah** — our passage says only תחות כורסייא, and identifying the Throne
  with Binah is what makes the ר"ז / ק"ג split structural;
- the 207 of the right are called **אור קדמאה**, the First Light, and per the
  **סולם** it is a dim light that produces no offspring — the pedigree of the
  right/left asymmetry the Arizal then applies to Esau's side;
- Frish's footnotes there give the עץ חיים cross-references.

### The number 310 outside the Zohar

- **משנה עוקצין ג:יב / סנהדרין ק ע"א** — ש"י עולמות given to each tzaddik, read
  out of `להנחיל אהבי יש` (Prov. 8:21). Already noted above as context.
- There is **no single accepted explanation** of the figure. Other readings in
  circulation: בראשית as ברא ש"י; תורת חיים — man and wife together merit 620
  and divide them, 310 each; בעל הסולם — written and oral Torah, 2 × 310 = 620.
  Worth knowing before teaching it again; none of these is in our passage.
