# Vayetze (Genesis 28:10–32:3)

## Progress

Carried over from the earlier claude.ai sessions. **These are the session's own
summary of what was covered — the sentence-by-sentence notes themselves were not
saved and are not reproduced here.** The scan (`Vayetze/Scans/Vayetze.pdf`,
15 pages) has not been copied into the repo yet.

In that scan, PDF pages 1 and 2 are the same book page (שפט).

### Covered

1. **Book page שפט, top** — the end of a passage that begins on page שפח (not
   scanned). Jacob as Yesod of Abba emerging from Yesod of Binah (באר שבע);
   4+3 and 3+4 = 7 mochin; חרן = 3 × אלהים.

2. **ליקוטי תורה, "ויצא יעקב מבאר שבע וילך חרנה"** (bottom of שפט → top of שצ).
   Points the Arizal makes:
   - ס"ת of ויצא יעקב מבאר = אבר; reversed = באר.
   - 7 Havayot = יעקב (182).
   - גרון = 3 × אלהים = חרן; נחר = גרון with the kolel.
   - חרנה: the ה = the 5 letters of אלהים.
   - Three Elokim (yuds for Leah, alefs for Jacob, heis for Rachel).
   - יעקב = 2 × אלהים (עקב) plus the י for the 10 letters.

3. **שער הפסוקים, "ויצא יעקב... ירמוז על יציאת מצרים וקבלת התורה"**
   (page שצ, right column → top of middle column). Points the Arizal makes:
   - The stone on the well = the kushiyot and terutzim of Torah study, removed
     by Jacob in עקבי משיחא.
   - The 4 letters of הויה in the tablets: 10 commandments, 5 on each tablet,
     6 × 6 handbreadths.
   - 36 per tablet; together ע"ב = עב, "thickness," as in בעב הענן.
